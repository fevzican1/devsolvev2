## How to verify progress

- **Bing Webmaster Tools → Backlinks** — watch the "Referring Domains" count
  climb as the above get indexed.
- **Google Search Console → Links → External links** — same idea on Google.
- The warning clears once a handful of distinct high-quality referring domains
  point at devsolvev2.com. Quality (relevance + domain authority) matters far
  more than quantity.
